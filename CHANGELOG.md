# What's new in Dustoff

## 0.1.3: Discord shows what you're playing (2026-11-13)

- Your Discord status now says the name of the game you're playing, like "Playing New Super Mario Bros. U", with its box art and how long you've been playing.
- When friends join with "Play with a friend", your status says how many are playing with you.
- Discord no longer just says "Cemu" or "Dolphin" while you play.
- Don't want it? Turn off "Show what I'm playing on Discord" in Settings.
- New "What's new" list in Settings. Click any version to see what changed.

## 0.1.2: Play with a friend (2026-11-13)

- New "Play with a friend" button. Send your friend a short code and they can join your game over the internet with their own controller. They don't need the game.
- Dustoff now updates itself. When a new version is out, you'll get a message and can update from Settings with one click.
- The Library shows how many players each game supports.
- The Channels look now shows 12 games per page and slides between pages.

## 0.1.1: Meet the mascot (2026-11-11)

- Say hi to the Dustoff dust cloud. It shows up in messages, while games start, and when your library is empty.
- Dustoff has its own icon on the taskbar and desktop.
- The version number is now shown in Settings.

## 0.1.0: First release (2026-11-10)

- Drag your games onto Dustoff and they're sorted, named and given box art for you.
- Plays NES, SNES, N64, Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, GameCube, Wii and Wii U games.
- Made for a controller: browse, search and play without touching the mouse.
- Continue right where you left off, and open one menu during any game to save or quit.
- RetroAchievements and play time tracking.
- Change your buttons for every system, or for one game.
- Ten looks to choose from in the Theme tab.
